# Privacy Policy for SSMC Bot

**Last Updated: 6 September 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the **SSMC Discord Bot** ("SSMC", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Bot.

By adding, using, or interacting with the SSMC Bot, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We May Collect

The SSMC Bot may collect and process limited information required for its functionality. This may include:

* Discord User IDs
* Discord Server (Guild) IDs
* Discord Channel IDs
* Discord Role IDs
* Discord Message IDs
* Command usage and interaction data
* Information voluntarily submitted through commands, forms, applications, reports, or other Bot features
* Server configuration and settings created through the Bot

The Bot does not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information collected by the Bot may be used to:

* Provide and operate Bot features
* Store server-specific settings and configurations
* Process applications, reports, requests, or submissions
* Provide moderation and administration features
* Prevent abuse, spam, or malicious use
* Improve the functionality and reliability of the Bot
* Maintain logs where a server has enabled logging features

Information is only used where reasonably necessary for the Bot to function.

## 3. Message Content

The SSMC Bot does not intentionally store the contents of Discord messages unless this is required by a specific feature, such as:

* Moderation systems
* Reports
* Applications
* Logging systems
* Commands or interactions where information is voluntarily submitted

Where message content is processed, it is only used for the functionality of the relevant Bot feature.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Data may be stored in a database or other secure storage system operated by the Bot developers.

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may only be disclosed when:

* Required to provide the Bot's functionality
* Required by applicable law
* Necessary to protect the security or integrity of the Bot
* Necessary to investigate abuse, fraud, or malicious activity

## 6. Third-Party Services

The SSMC Bot operates through Discord and may use third-party services or APIs where required for certain features.

Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

## 7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's functionality or maintain server configurations, logs, records, and other relevant features.

Server administrators may request the removal of server-specific data where technically possible and appropriate.

Users may also request the deletion of personal data associated with the Bot, subject to legitimate operational, security, or legal requirements.
